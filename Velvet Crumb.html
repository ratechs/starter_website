Our story

Baked with love. Crafted with care.

Velvet Crumb began in a home kitchen with one oven, a borrowed mixer and a neighbour's birthday cake that was meant to be simple. It wasn't. We kept the oven, added a small team, and never stopped baking every cake to order.

We use cultured butter, Belgian couverture, whole vanilla pods and fruit in season. No premixes, no essences, no shortcuts. Each cake is designed with you, because the best celebrations are the ones that look like the person they are for.
